Sudanizer

Live

Tokenization engine for Sudanese dialects.

Sudanizer is a text tokenization and segmentation engine built specifically to process Sudanese dialects and to study how distinctive cultural words and expressions are represented, in support of research on Sudanese dialects.

Get involved
Category
Research & Language Technology
Languages
Arabic
Built for
Researchers and teams working with Sudanese dialect text.

The problem

General-purpose tokenizers split Sudanese dialect words into many small fragments. In our public benchmark, a widely used tokenizer needs more than four tokens per word, and distinctive cultural words are broken apart.

How Sudanizer tackles it

Sudanizer is a byte-pair encoding tokenizer trained at scale on Sudanese dialect vocabulary, with a wide vocabulary for cultural expressions and careful handling of word forms, emoji and spelling variation.

What it does

  • Trained at scale on a database of Sudanese dialect vocabulary.
  • A wide vocabulary designed to carry cultural expressions and distinctive words.
  • Careful handling of word forms, emoji and spelling variation.

How it works

The path a user takes through Sudanizer, step by step.

  1. Input

    Raw Sudanese dialect text, as people write it.

  2. Normalize

    Spelling variation, word forms and emoji are handled.

  3. Segment

    Text is split into dialect-aware subword tokens.

  4. Use

    Tokens feed language models and dialect research.
